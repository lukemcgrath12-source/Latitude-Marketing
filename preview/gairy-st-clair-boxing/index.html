Meet your coach

Superman, now in your corner

Gairy "Superman" St Clair won the IBF and IBO super-featherweight world titles in 2006 and was inducted into the Australian National Boxing Hall of Fame in 2022. Now he brings that same craft to everyday people in Gymea: stance, footwork, real technique, and a workout that gets you fitter than you thought you could be.

You don't need to be fit to start. Just turn up and keep coming.
