How do I book a service or repair?

Fill in the form below or call Mohammed on +679 804 1667. Tell us what's happening and we'll get you booked in.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We look after air conditioning and refrigeration for shops, restaurants and businesses, as well as homes.

Should I repair or replace my unit?

It depends on the age and the fault. We'll check it and give you an honest answer, with a price for each option.
