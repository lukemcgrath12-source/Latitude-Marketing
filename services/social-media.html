Content That Builds Trust While You Work

Posting is not the problem. Most business owners either go quiet for six weeks and then post three times in a day, or post constantly and hear nothing back. The Problem

Why Posting Alone Isn't Working

If you're putting time into content and getting nothing back, it's usually one of these, and usually more than one.

  • Organic reach is a fraction of what it used to be. Posting to your followers now reaches a small slice of them.
  • The content is about the business rather than the customer, so there's nothing in it for the person scrolling.
  • Inconsistent posting resets how the platform reads your account every single time you go quiet.
  • There's no visual system, so two posts side by side don't look like they came from the same business.
  • Nothing on the page proves the work. No finished jobs, no reviews, no faces.
  • There's no distribution behind any of it. Content with no reach is a diary, not marketing.

Where Content Sits

Trust And Reach Are Two Different Jobs

The most common mistake is expecting one to do the other's work.

Organic Content Builds Trust

It's what someone looks at after they've heard your name. A live, current, professional looking feed is often the difference between a referral turning into a call and quietly going elsewhere. What it will not do on its own is put you in front of people who've never heard of you.

Paid Advertising Builds Reach

Ads are how you get seen by people outside your existing circle. They work far better when there's something credible waiting when that person checks your profile. Content and paid ads are two halves of the same job.

Questions

Straight Answers

How often should we be posting?

Consistency matters more than volume. A steady two or three posts a week that actually go out beats a plan for daily content that collapses in month two. We'd rather set a cadence you can sustain and build from there.

Do I need to be on camera?

No, but it helps. For most local trades and service businesses, people are buying the person as much as the service. If you're not comfortable on camera, the work itself, the finished jobs, and the team can carry it instead.

Do you use my photos or stock images?

Yours, wherever we can get them. Photos of your actual jobs, your van, your team, and your finished work consistently outperform anything bought off a stock library. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot on your phone and when.

Will content on its own get me enquiries?

Sometimes, and slowly. Content is what makes people trust you once they've found you. If nobody is finding you in the first place, the honest answer is that content isn't your first problem and we'll say so.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Everything is month to month. The intention is that you stay because the work is worth it.
