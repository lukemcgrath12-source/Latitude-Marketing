The Problem

Where Small Business Ad Spend Goes Wrong

Most business owners who tell us "we tried ads and they didn't work" ran into one of these. The platform wasn't the issue.

  • Boosting posts instead of running a structured campaign. Boosting optimises for engagement, not enquiries.
  • No conversion tracking in place, so the platform has no idea what a good outcome looks like and can't optimise toward it.
  • Traffic sent to a homepage instead of a page built for the thing the ad promised.
  • A small budget split across four campaigns, so none of them ever gather enough data to get out of the learning phase.
  • Set and forget. Campaigns launched, then left alone for three months while performance drifts.
  • No idea which enquiries came from ads, so the whole thing gets judged on a feeling.

Choosing A Channel

Google And Meta Do Different Jobs

Which one you start with depends on whether people go looking for what you do, or need reminding that you exist.

Google Ads Captures Intent

Someone typing "emergency roof repair Sutherland Shire" at nine at night has already decided to buy. They're choosing between whoever appears. Google Ads puts you in that consideration set. Best for urgent, searched for services: trades, repairs, emergency work, anything with a defined problem behind it.

Meta Ads Creates Demand

Facebook and Instagram put you in front of people in your area who fit your customer profile before they need you. Best for considered purchases, retail, seasonal work, and building local recognition so your name is familiar when the need does come up.

Questions

Straight Answers

What budget do I actually need?

Enough for one campaign to gather real data, rather than enough to spread across several. Splitting a small budget is the single most common reason local ad accounts underperform. We'd rather run one campaign properly than four badly, and we'll tell you on the call if the number you have in mind isn't going to move.

How long before I see results?

Google can produce enquiries in the first fortnight because you're catching people already searching. Meta usually takes longer, since it's building awareness before it converts. Either way, the first few weeks are partly the platform learning, and judging performance too early is how good campaigns get switched off.

Do I need a decent website first?

Generally yes. Ads amplify whatever the page already does. If the page doesn't convert, ads just buy you more people leaving. If that's the situation, we'd usually recommend fixing the website first rather than taking your ad spend.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Accounts are set up in your name with us given access, not the other way around. Pixel data, conversion history, and audiences stay with you.

Is the ad spend separate from your fee?

Yes. Ad spend goes directly to Google and Meta on your own card. Our fee covers the management, creative, and reporting.
