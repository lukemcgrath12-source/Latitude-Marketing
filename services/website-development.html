The Problem

Why Most Small Business Sites Don't Convert

A site can look completely fine and still cost you work every week. These are the issues we find most often when we audit a site on a discovery call.

  • It loads slowly on a phone, which is where most of your traffic comes from.
  • There's no obvious next step, so people read, nod, and leave.
  • There's no proof on it. No real photos, no reviews, no jobs you've actually done.
  • It was built on a template that was never set up to be found in search.
  • Nobody has touched it in three years, so the services listed aren't the ones you offer now.
  • Enquiries go to an email address you don't check, or the form quietly stopped working.

The Build

What's Involved

Every build runs through the same six stages. Typical timeline is three to four weeks from kickoff, depending on how quickly photos and content come back.

01

Structure and Strategy

We map what your customers actually need to see, and in what order, before anything gets designed. Page structure is decided here, not halfway through.

02

Design and Build

Custom, mobile first, and fast. Built to load quickly on a phone with patchy reception, because that's the situation most of your enquiries come from.

03

Copy That Does The Work

Written around the questions your customers actually ask, in plain language. Not industry jargon, and not filler written to hit a word count.

04

Search Foundations

Clean URL structure, unique page titles, local search signals, and schema markup so Google can work out who you are, what you do, and where you do it.

05

Enquiry Tracking

Forms, click to call, and your Google Business Profile connected up, so you can see where the work is coming from instead of guessing.

06

Launch and Handover

Live, indexed, and documented. The domain, the hosting, and the site itself stay in your name. You are not renting your own website.

Questions

Straight Answers

How long does a website take?

Three to four weeks is typical, measured from kickoff. The variable is almost always how quickly photos, service details, and sign off come back from your side. Nothing goes live until you've seen it and signed it off.

Do I own the site once it's done?

Yes. Domain, hosting, and the site all stay in your name. If you ever want to move it or hand it to someone else, you can. There's no lock in, and engagements are month to month.

Will it rank on Google straight away?

No, and anyone promising that is guessing. A new site has no history. What we do is build the technical foundations properly and get your Google Business Profile set up, so local searches have something to find from day one. Ranking builds from there over weeks and months.

Do I need a new site, or can the current one be fixed?

Depends. Sometimes the structure is sound and it needs copy, speed work, and a proper enquiry path. Sometimes the platform itself is the ceiling. That's what the discovery call is for, and the honest answer is occasionally that you don't need us yet.

Do you only work with Sutherland Shire businesses?

Most clients are in the Shire and southern Sydney, which is where the local knowledge counts. The work itself isn't location bound.
