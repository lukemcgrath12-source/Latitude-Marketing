Common questions

How long does a website take?

Three to four weeks is typical from kickoff. The variable is almost always how quickly photos, service details, and sign off come back from your side. Nothing goes live until you have seen it and approved it.

Do I own the site once it's done?

Yes. Domain, hosting, and the site all stay in your name. There is no lock in and engagements run month to month.

Will it rank on Google straight away?

No, and anyone promising that is guessing. A new site has no history. We build the technical foundations properly and get your Google Business Profile set up so local searches have something to find. Ranking builds from there over weeks and months.

Do I need a new site, or can the current one be fixed?

Depends. Sometimes the structure is sound and it just needs copy, speed work, and a proper enquiry path. Sometimes the platform itself is the ceiling. That is what the discovery call is for, and occasionally the honest answer is that you do not need us yet.

Do you only work with Sutherland Shire businesses?

Most clients are in the Shire and southern Sydney, which is where the local knowledge counts. The work itself is not location bound.
