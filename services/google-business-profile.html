Google Business Profile & Local SEO

Show Up When Someone Nearby Searches

For most local service businesses, Google Business Profile brings in more enquiries than the website and social put together. It's the map result, the reviews, the phone number, and the "open now". Most businesses either never claimed it, or set it up once and never touched it again.

The Problem

What An Unmanaged Profile Costs You

This is the cheapest fix on the list and the one most often left sitting there. Every item below is something we see weekly.

  • You don't appear in the map results for your own suburb, so three competitors get the call before anyone reaches your name.
  • Hours are wrong or missing, so someone rings a business that shows as open instead of one that shows as closed.
  • Barely any reviews, or reviews sitting there with no response from you.
  • No photos, so the listing looks like a business that shut down two years ago.
  • No service areas set, which means you're invisible the moment someone searches from two suburbs over.
  • Categories and services never filled in, so Google has no idea which searches you should appear for.

The Work

What's Involved

Set up properly once, then maintained. The maintenance is where the ranking actually comes from.

01

Claim and Verify

Getting you verified ownership of the listing, including sorting out duplicate or unclaimed listings that are splitting your reviews.

02

Full Profile Build

Primary and secondary categories, every service listed, service areas mapped to the suburbs you actually work in, hours, attributes, and a description written for search rather than filler.

03

Photos

Real photos of your work, your team, and your vehicles, uploaded and kept current. Listings with recent photos get engaged with far more than ones without.

04

Review System

A simple, repeatable way to ask every happy customer for a review, plus responses written for the ones that come in. Reviews are one of the strongest local ranking signals you can influence.

05

Ongoing Posts

Regular Google posts covering jobs, offers, and updates. An active profile outranks a dormant one, and this is the part almost everyone skips.

06

Local SEO On Your Site

Location relevant pages, consistent business name, address and phone number across the web, and LocalBusiness schema so your site and your listing reinforce each other.

Worth Understanding

The Map Pack Is A Separate Race

Ranking in the map results and ranking in the blue links underneath are two different competitions with different rules.

Map Results Run On Proximity, Relevance And Prominence

How close you are to the searcher, how well your categories and services match what they typed, and how established you look through reviews, activity, and mentions elsewhere. Your website matters here, but your profile matters more.

Organic Results Run On Your Site

Page structure, content, speed, and links. Slower to move, and this is where having proper pages for each service starts to pay off. The two feed each other, which is why we treat the website and the profile as one job.

Questions

Straight Answers

Can I just do this myself?

Yes, and some businesses do it well. The setup is not complicated. Where it falls over is the maintenance: posts, photos, and reviews week after week for months. If you'll genuinely keep that up, do it yourself and put the money into something else.

How long until I show up in the map pack?

Depends on how competitive your category is and how far you are from the centre of the area you're targeting. Some categories move within weeks. Others take months of consistent activity and review growth. Anyone quoting you a fixed timeframe is making it up.

How many reviews do I need?

More than the businesses currently ranking above you, and preferably coming in steadily rather than twenty in one week. Recency counts. A profile with forty reviews where the last one was two years ago looks worse than one with fifteen from this month.

I don't have a shopfront. Does this still apply?

Yes, and it matters more. Service area businesses can hide the address and list the suburbs they cover instead. Getting those service areas set correctly is one of the highest impact changes we make.

Should I do this before running ads?

Usually. It's the cheapest source of local enquiries available and it works while your paid campaigns are still finding their feet.
