Common questions

Can I just do this myself?

Yes, and some businesses do it well. The setup is not complicated. Where it falls over is the maintenance: posts, photos, and reviews week after week for months. If you will genuinely keep that up, do it yourself and put the money elsewhere.

How long until I show up in the map pack?

Depends on how competitive your category is and how far you are from the centre of the area you are targeting. Some categories move within weeks, others take months of consistent activity. Anyone quoting a fixed timeframe is making it up.

How many reviews do I need?

More than the businesses currently ranking above you, and preferably coming in steadily rather than twenty in one week. Recency counts. Forty reviews where the last was two years ago looks worse than fifteen from this month.

I don't have a shopfront. Does this still apply?

Yes, and it matters more. Service area businesses can hide the address and list the suburbs they cover instead. Getting those service areas set correctly is one of the highest impact changes we make.

Should I do this before running ads?

Usually. It is the cheapest source of local enquiries available and it keeps working while paid campaigns are still finding their feet.
