About Latitude

REAL EXPERIENCE. REAL RESULTS.

A decade of marketing at the highest level of Australian sport and media. now working for local businesses in the Sutherland Shire and across Sydney.

The Founder

LUKE MCGRATH

Luke McGrath is a Sydney-based marketer with over a decade working across some of Australia's most recognised sport and entertainment brands. Before starting Latitude Marketing, he held roles at the Cronulla-Sutherland Sharks, the Australian Turf Club, and Tabcorp — running campaigns, managing brands, and driving real commercial outcomes.

Latitude was built to bring that same level of thinking to local businesses in Sydney — businesses that are great at what they do, but aren't getting found online. The difference with Latitude is simple: you work directly with Luke. No handoffs, no account managers, no offshore execution. Just honest strategy and clean delivery.

Luke still works full-time in sport marketing at the Cronulla Sharks, which means every insight, trend, and tool he uses with clients is being tested at a professional level — not just read about in a blog post.
