Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

  1. Who we are

    Latitude Marketing is a trading name of Luke McGrath, based in the Sutherland Shire, NSW. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact us at info@latitudemarketing.au.

  2. What we collect

    Your name, business name, suburb, email address, phone number, website or social media details, and any answers you give in our forms. We collect this when you fill in a form on our website or on Facebook or Instagram, message us, enter a giveaway, or become a client.

  3. Why we collect it

    To reply to your enquiry, prepare anything you asked for (such as a visibility check, score or quote), deliver our services and send you information about them. We only contact you about Latitude Marketing.

  4. Who we share it with

    We don't sell your information. We use trusted tools to run our business, such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram lead forms), Google, our email provider and our project management tools, and your details may be stored on their servers, including overseas. We share information if the law requires it.

  5. Website analytics

    Our website uses analytics and advertising tools, including Vercel Web Analytics, Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, to understand how the site is used and to measure our ads. These tools may use cookies. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

  6. Marketing messages

    If we send you marketing emails or messages you can opt out at any time by replying "stop" or using the unsubscribe link.

  7. Keeping it safe

    We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete or de-identify it when we no longer need it.

  8. Access and corrections

    You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@latitudemarketing.au. We'll respond within 30 days.

  9. Complaints

    If you have a privacy concern, email us first and we'll work to resolve it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

  10. Changes

    We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page. Last updated October 2026.
